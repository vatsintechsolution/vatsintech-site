How Can Business Central Streamline Jewellery Production Planning?

Jewellery manufacturing is fundamentally different from conventional manufacturing. Production may involve precious metals, gemstones, intricate designs, multiple production stages, subcontracting, quality checks, wastage, and highly variable customer requirements.

For jewellery manufacturers, production planning is therefore not simply about deciding what to produce and when. It also involves understanding material availability, production capacity, component requirements, expected delivery dates, and the cost associated with every production order.

This is where Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central can help.

Business Central provides manufacturing capabilities such as production bills of materials, routings, production orders, capacity planning, inventory management, and supply planning. These capabilities can help jewellery businesses create more structured production processes and improve visibility from material planning through finished goods.

Quick Answer: How Does Business Central Help Jewellery Production Planning?

Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central can streamline jewellery production planning by connecting production orders, bills of materials, routings, inventory, material requirements, capacity, purchasing, and costing within one ERP system.

For jewellery manufacturers, this can help with:

  • Production planning
  • Material requirement planning
  • Bill of Materials management
  • Routing and operation planning
  • Raw-material availability
  • Production order management
  • Capacity planning
  • Work-in-progress visibility
  • Inventory control
  • Subcontracting
  • Production costing
  • Demand-based replenishment
  • Financial integration
  • Production reporting

The exact processes and configuration required will depend on the jewellery manufacturer’s operating model, product complexity, and industry-specific requirements.

Why Is Production Planning Important for Jewellery Manufacturers?

Jewellery production often involves multiple stages before a finished piece reaches the customer.

A typical process may involve:

Design → Material Planning → Casting/Forming → Stone Setting → Polishing → Finishing → Quality Check → Finished Goods

Each stage can involve different resources, materials, workers, machines, and quality requirements.

A delay at one stage can affect the entire production schedule.

For example, if the required gold alloy or gemstones are unavailable, production cannot proceed as planned. Similarly, if a particular work center is overloaded, the expected completion date may shift.

This makes production planning for jewellery manufacturing highly dependent on accurate information.

A centralized ERP can help bring production, inventory, purchasing, and financial information together instead of managing them through disconnected spreadsheets and manual processes.

1. Create Structured Production Bills of Materials

A Production Bill of Materials (BOM) defines the components and quantities required to manufacture an item.

For jewellery manufacturers, a BOM can be used to structure the materials and components associated with a finished product.

Depending on the business process, this may include:

  • Precious metals
  • Gemstones
  • Other components
  • Consumables
  • Packaging materials
  • Subassemblies

Business Central supports production BOMs as part of its manufacturing functionality. A well-maintained BOM provides the production team with a structured view of what is required to manufacture a product.

For jewellery businesses, accurate BOM management can also support better material planning and costing.

Key benefit:
Better visibility into material requirements before production begins.

2. Plan Production Based on Material Availability

One of the biggest challenges in jewellery manufacturing is ensuring that required materials are available when production starts.

A production plan is only useful if the business has access to the required materials.

Business Central can connect production planning with inventory and supply planning so organizations can identify material requirements and plan procurement or production accordingly.

Its manufacturing capabilities include Material Requirements Planning (MRP) and supply planning, which can help determine what needs to be purchased or produced based on demand and inventory conditions.

This can help jewellery manufacturers reduce situations such as:

  • Production delays caused by material shortages
  • Excess inventory
  • Unplanned purchasing
  • Emergency procurement
  • Production interruptions

3. Manage Jewellery Production Orders

Production orders provide a structured way to manage manufacturing activities.

Instead of relying on informal production instructions, manufacturers can create production orders that define what needs to be produced and manage material consumption and output.

Business Central supports production orders as a core manufacturing capability.

For jewellery manufacturers, production orders can help connect:

Customer/Demand → Production Requirement → Material Consumption → Manufacturing Operations → Finished Product

This provides better visibility into production status and helps teams understand what is currently being manufactured.

4. Improve Production Scheduling With Routings

Jewellery manufacturing can involve several operations.

For example:

Casting → Grinding → Setting → Polishing → Finishing → Quality Inspection

A routing defines the sequence of operations required to manufacture a product.

Business Central supports routings, work centers, and machine centers, allowing manufacturers to structure production operations and resources.

For jewellery manufacturers, properly defined routings can help answer important questions:

  • What operation comes next?
  • Which resource is required?
  • How much time is expected?
  • Where is the production order currently positioned?
  • Which work center is responsible?

This creates a more structured production environment.

5. Balance Production Capacity

A production schedule can fail if the required resources are not available.

For example, a jewellery manufacturer may have enough raw material but limited capacity at a particular work center.

Business Central supports capacity planning and finite capacity scheduling, helping manufacturers plan production around available resources rather than simply creating schedules without considering capacity.

This can help manufacturers identify:

  • Resource constraints
  • Overloaded work centers
  • Capacity gaps
  • Production bottlenecks
  • Potential scheduling conflicts

For jewellery businesses with multiple production stages, this can be particularly useful.

6. Improve Raw Material and Inventory Visibility

Jewellery manufacturers deal with inventory that can have significant financial value.

A lack of inventory visibility can therefore create operational and financial challenges.

Business Central supports inventory management, multiple locations, lot and serial tracking, warehouse processes, and supply planning.

Depending on the organization’s configuration and specific jewellery requirements, manufacturers can use Business Central to create better visibility into:

  • Raw materials
  • Components
  • Work-in-progress
  • Finished jewellery
  • Warehouse inventory
  • Production consumption
  • Material movements

For high-value inventory, accurate records and controlled processes are especially important.

7. Improve Work-in-Progress Visibility

Jewellery products may move through multiple stages before becoming finished goods.

Without structured production tracking, management may struggle to understand:

What is currently in production?

Which stage is it at?

How much material has been consumed?

When will it be completed?

Business Central’s production functionality can help connect production orders, consumption, output, and costing information.

This creates a stronger foundation for monitoring work in progress (WIP) and understanding production status.

8. Support Jewellery Job Work and Subcontracting

Many jewellery manufacturers use external parties for specific production processes.

For example, a business may outsource:

  • Casting
  • Stone setting
  • Polishing
  • Plating
  • Specialized finishing

Business Central supports subcontracting within its manufacturing capabilities.

A properly designed Business Central implementation can help organizations bring subcontracted activities into the broader production planning process.

This can improve visibility into:

Material Sent → External Operation → Material Returned → Further Processing → Finished Goods

The exact subcontracting workflow should be designed around the jewellery manufacturer’s actual business process.

9. Improve Jewellery Production Costing

Jewellery manufacturing costs can involve much more than raw materials.

Businesses may need to consider:

  • Precious metals
  • Gemstones
  • Labour
  • Machine time
  • Subcontracting
  • Overheads
  • Wastage
  • Processing costs
  • Finishing costs

Business Central provides manufacturing and cost-accounting capabilities that can help businesses track production costs and analyze variances. Dynamics Square’s manufacturing guidance highlights standard versus actual costing, WIP accounting, and job costing as relevant manufacturing practices.

For jewellery businesses, this can support better understanding of the cost associated with manufacturing each product or production order.

10. Connect Production With Purchasing

Production planning and procurement are closely connected.

If the production plan identifies that a required material is unavailable, the purchasing team needs to know what is required and when.

Business Central can connect supply planning with purchasing and inventory processes.

This can help businesses create a more coordinated workflow:

Demand → Material Requirement → Inventory Check → Purchase/Production Planning → Material Availability → Production

This reduces the need for production and procurement teams to maintain separate spreadsheets and manually reconcile requirements.

11. Support Demand-Based Production Planning

Jewellery demand can change based on:

  • Seasonal collections
  • Festivals
  • Weddings
  • Retail demand
  • Customer orders
  • Market trends
  • New designs
  • Promotional campaigns

Business Central’s planning capabilities can help organizations use demand and inventory information to plan supply requirements.

Forecasting and MRP can help manufacturers align production and procurement decisions with expected requirements.

This can help businesses avoid two common problems:

Overproduction → unnecessary inventory and capital tied up in stock.

Underproduction → missed customer demand and delayed orders.

12. Connect Production With Finance

One of the biggest advantages of ERP-based production planning is that manufacturing does not have to operate separately from finance.

When production, inventory, purchasing, sales, and financial transactions are connected, management can gain a more complete view of business performance.

Business Central is designed as an integrated business management platform covering finance, supply chain, sales, inventory, and manufacturing capabilities.

For jewellery businesses, this can help management connect operational decisions with financial outcomes.

13. Use Dashboards and Reporting for Production Decisions

Production planning is more effective when managers can measure performance.

Useful manufacturing KPIs can include:

  • Production output
  • Production lead time
  • Material consumption
  • Production cost
  • Capacity utilization
  • Order completion
  • Inventory levels
  • WIP
  • Scrap/wastage
  • On-time delivery

Business Central can be extended with reporting and analytics tools such as Power BI. Dynamics Square specifically highlights Power BI dashboards for manufacturing KPIs and production insights.

This allows management to move from:

“What happened?”

to:

“Why did it happen?”

and ultimately:

“What should we change?”

Best Practices for Jewellery Production Planning With Business Central

Start With Accurate Master Data

Before implementing an ERP, review:

  • Item masters
  • BOMs
  • Routings
  • Units of measure
  • Work centers
  • Vendors
  • Customers
  • Inventory records

Poor master data can create inaccurate production plans.

Standardize Production Processes

Document how each major jewellery product moves through production.

Then configure Business Central around the standardized process wherever possible.

Define Accurate BOMs

Incorrect BOM quantities can lead to incorrect material planning and costing.

Maintain Accurate Routings

Production routings should reflect the actual sequence of operations.

Review Capacity Regularly

Production schedules should consider available machine and labour capacity.

Connect Procurement With Planning

Purchasing teams should have visibility into materials required for upcoming production.

Monitor Production KPIs

Regularly review production performance to identify bottlenecks and inefficiencies.

These principles align with the manufacturing best-practice approach emphasized in Dynamics Square’s Business Central content, particularly around accurate BOMs/routings, capacity planning, inventory planning, costing, data, automation, and continuous improvement.

Common Challenges When Implementing Business Central for Jewellery Manufacturing

Complex Product Structures

Jewellery products can have highly variable material and component requirements.

Approach: Build accurate and maintainable BOM structures.

High-Value Inventory

Precious metals and gemstones require strong inventory controls.

Approach: Design appropriate tracking, warehouse, approval, and reconciliation processes.

Production Variability

Custom jewellery may not follow the same production process as standardized products.

Approach: Design flexible production workflows that support the organization’s actual manufacturing model.

Subcontracting

External processes can make production visibility more difficult.

Approach: Include subcontracting within the overall production and material flow.

Data Migration

Legacy inventory, item, BOM, and vendor data may be inconsistent.

Approach: Clean and validate master data before migration.

Data migration and complex production processes are also identified as major manufacturing ERP implementation challenges in Dynamics Square’s current manufacturing guidance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Business Central be used for jewellery manufacturing?

Yes. Business Central includes manufacturing capabilities such as production BOMs, routings, production orders, capacity planning, inventory management, and supply planning. However, jewellery-specific requirements may require configuration or industry extensions depending on the business process.

How does Business Central help with jewellery production planning?

It can connect production orders with BOMs, routings, inventory, supply planning, and capacity information, helping manufacturers create more structured production plans.

Can Business Central manage jewellery inventory?

Business Central provides inventory management capabilities, including multi-location inventory and lot/serial tracking. The exact tracking model for precious metals and gemstones should be designed according to the jewellery company’s operational and regulatory requirements.

Can Business Central manage subcontracted jewellery processes?

Business Central supports subcontracting within its manufacturing functionality, making it possible to incorporate outsourced operations into production workflows.

Can Business Central help calculate jewellery manufacturing costs?

Business Central provides manufacturing and costing capabilities that can help organizations track material, labour, overhead, WIP, and production-related costs. The exact costing model should be configured according to the organization’s requirements.

Does Business Central support production capacity planning?

Yes. Business Central supports capacity planning and finite capacity scheduling capabilities for manufacturing.

Can Business Central integrate with Power BI?

Yes. Business Central can work with Power BI for data visualization and reporting, enabling organizations to build dashboards and analyze business performance.
