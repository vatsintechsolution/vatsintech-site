Top 10 Third-Party Apps That Perfectly Integrate with Business Central

 

Supercharge Your ERP with These Must-Have Add-Ons

Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central is a robust ERP solution—but its real power comes alive when integrated with the right third-party applications. From automating finance operations to streamlining eCommerce, these integrations help businesses work smarter, faster, and more accurately.

In this blog, we explore the top 10 third-party apps that integrate seamlessly with Dynamics 365 Business Central in 2025.

 Must-Have Third-Party Apps for Dynamics 365 Business Central

1. Continia Document Capture – AP Automation

Automate invoice processing and document management

Keyword: Dynamics 365 Business Central AP automation
Continia uses OCR to scan, read, and import invoice data directly into Business Central, reducing manual entry.

Features:

  • OCR-based invoice scanning

  • Digital archiving and approval workflows

  • Seamless AP automation

2. Shopify Connector by Microsoft – eCommerce Sync

Keep your online store and ERP in sync

Keyword: Dynamics 365 Business Central Shopify integration
Sync your Shopify store with Business Central for real-time order, product, and inventory management.

Features:

  • Automated order import

  • Real-time inventory updates

  • Centralized customer data

3. Jet Reports – Financial Reporting in Excel

Advanced reporting inside Microsoft Excel

Keyword: Jet Reports for Dynamics 365 Business Central
Jet Reports lets you build dynamic, live reports using Business Central data—all within Excel.

Features:

  • Drag-and-drop report builder

  • Live ERP data in Excel

  • Financial and operational templates

4. Sana Commerce – B2B eCommerce Integration

Real-time ERP-powered online storefronts

Keyword: Dynamics 365 Business Central B2B eCommerce
Sana enables accurate product info, real-time stock levels, and customer-specific pricing on your B2B web store.

Features:

  • Live ERP product and pricing data

  • Personalized customer portals

  • Full order-to-cash visibility

5. Tasklet Factory Mobile WMS – Warehouse Management

Empower warehouse teams with mobile tools

Keyword: warehouse management for Dynamics 365 Business Central
Tasklet Factory delivers mobile barcode scanning and offline functionality for warehouse operations.

Features:

  • Mobile inventory management

  • Offline mode support

  • Real-time data sync

6. Versapay Payments – Integrated Payment Gateway

Streamline secure credit card payments

Keyword: payment processing Dynamics 365 Business Central
ChargeLogic allows PCI-compliant credit card processing directly from within Business Central.

Features:

  • Multi-currency support

  • Secure, encrypted payment flows

  • Auto-generation of receipts

7. Avalara AvaTax – Tax Compliance Automation

Simplify tax calculations and reporting

Keyword: tax automation for Dynamics 365 Business Central
Avalara automates tax calculations based on location, product, and jurisdiction.

Features:

  • Real-time sales tax calculation

  • Integration with filing systems

  • Compliance in multiple regions

8. Click Dimensions – Marketing Automation for D365

Drive smarter customer engagement

Keyword: marketing automation Dynamics 365 Business Central
ClickDimensions connects your marketing workflows with Dynamics CRM for email, web, and social engagement.

Features:

  • Email campaign automation

  • Lead scoring and behavior tracking

  • Seamless CRM integration

9. LS Central – Retail Management Platform

End-to-end POS and ERP solution for retailers

Keyword: retail POS integration Dynamics 365
LS Central combines retail POS, loyalty programs, and inventory into one platform integrated with Business Central.

Features:

  • Mobile POS terminals

  • Loyalty and promotions engine

  • Unified inventory and pricing

10. Zeta Docs – Document Management & Delivery

Automate invoicing, approvals, and archiving

Keyword: document management for Dynamics 365 Business Central
ZetaDocs automates document delivery, invoice processing, and document storage—all linked to your ERP.

Features:

  • Email and PDF automation

  • Built-in approval workflows

  • Digital archiving for audits
