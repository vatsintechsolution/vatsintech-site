Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central Pricing in 2025: October Update

Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central pricing in 2025 depends on license types, user counts, and contract terms (annual vs. monthly). Below, we outline the updated costs, effective October 1, 2025, for new subscriptions and renewals, converted to INR (1 USD = 83.83 INR, June 18, 2025). Prices are per user per month unless specified, and Team Member licenses remain unchanged.

License Types and Costs

Essentials License
  • Current Price (until Sept 30, 2025): $70 (~₹5,868 INR) per user/month.
  • New Price (from Oct 1, 2025): $80 (~₹6,905 INR) per user/month.
  • Features: Covers financials, supply chain, sales, purchasing, inventory, and project management.
  • Ideal For: SMBs in retail or services needing core ERP functionality.
Premium License
  • Current Price: $100 (~₹8,383 INR) per user/month.
  • New Price: $110 (~₹9,221 INR) per user/month, a 10% increase.
  • Features: Includes Essentials features plus manufacturing and service management.
  • Ideal For: Distribution or service SMBs with advanced operational needs.
Device License
  • Current Price: $40 (~₹3,353 INR) per device/month.
  • New Price: $45 (~₹3,884 INR) per device/month, a 15% increase.
  • Features: Allows multiple users to share a single device for tasks like warehouse scanning.
  • Ideal For: SMBs with shared equipment in logistics or retail.
Team Member License
  • Price: $8 (~₹671 INR) per user/month (no change in 2025).
  • Features: Offers read-only access and limited write tasks (e.g., approvals, expense updates).
  • Ideal For: Executives or light users requiring basic access.

Additional Pricing Notes

  • Annual Contracts: Paid annually or monthly, with annual upfront payments avoiding a 5% increase for monthly billing.
  • Monthly Contracts: Incur a 20% premium over annual contracts, with a 5% increase from April 1, 2025, for monthly-paid annual commitments.
  • Promotions: Microsoft may offer discounts for new SMBs in 2025. A certified partner can negotiate these to lower costs.
  • Contact a Partner: Prices vary by region and currency. Reach out for tailored quotes for Indian SMBs.

Storage Increases in 2025

To offset the price hike, Microsoft is enhancing storage allocations per license, effective October 1, 2025, for new and renewed subscriptions. These upgrades support growing data demands from AI tools and analytics.

  • Essentials License: Increases from 2 GB to 3 GB per user (+50%).
  • Premium License: Increases from 3 GB to 5 GB per user (+67%).
  • Device License: Increases from 1 GB to 1.5 GB per device (+50%).
  • Team Member License: No storage increase specified, likely unchanged.

These enhancements accommodate larger datasets, such as sustainability reports or AI-driven inventory analytics, enabling SMBs to scale without additional storage fees.

Why the Price Increase in 2025?

Microsoft’s first Business Central price adjustment in over five years reflects significant platform improvements, justifying the increase:

  • AI and Copilot Integration: Copilot enables AI-powered automation, such as order creation from emails or financial reconciliation, at no extra cost.
  • Sustainability Module: Tracks carbon emissions and complies with regulations like EU CSRD, rolling out in 2024–2025.
  • Advanced Analytics: Provides real-time financial and operational insights with Power BI integration.
  • Interoperability: Enhances connections with Microsoft 365, Power Platform, and Azure for automation.
  • Global Compliance: Strengthens localizations for India, ensuring tax and regulatory compliance.
  • Master Data Management: Ensures cleaner, consistent data across departments.

These advancements position Business Central as a competitive ERP, aligning pricing with its expanded capabilities.

Additional Costs to Budget For

Beyond licenses, SMBs must plan for implementation and ongoing costs, which vary by business size and complexity.

Implementation Costs
  • Consulting Services: Includes configuration, data migration, and customization. A phased approach ensures cost transparency.
  • Data Migration: Involves transferring data from legacy systems (e.g., Tally, QuickBooks). Streamlined tools reduce expenses.
  • Customization: Covers tailored reports or integrations (e.g., e-commerce). Using native features minimizes costs.
Ongoing Costs
  • Training: Role-based sessions via Microsoft Learn or custom workshops to boost adoption.
  • Support: Post-go-live assistance for updates and compliance, with 24/7 availability.
  • Additional Storage: Beyond included allocations, extra storage incurs fees. Usage monitoring prevents surprises.

How to Prepare for the 2025 Price Increase

To mitigate the October 1, 2025, price hike and leverage storage upgrades, consider these strategies:

Lock in Current Pricing
  • Renew or extend subscriptions before September 30, 2025, for 1–3 years to retain current rates . A partner can assist with renewals.
Optimize License Usage
  • Review user roles: Assign Essentials/Premium to full users and Team Member licenses to light users. Audits reduced costs for a retail SMB by reassigning licenses.
  • Remove unused licenses to avoid overpaying.
Plan for Storage Needs
  • Assess data growth (e.g., from AI analytics). The new 3–5 GB allocations may suffice, with monitoring to prevent extra costs.
Budget for New Rates
  • Forecast the 10–15% increase from October 2025. For 10 Essentials users, costs rise from ₹58,680 to ₹64,550 INR/month. Request cost projections from a partner.
Explore Promotions
  • Microsoft’s 2025 SMB promotions may lower initial costs. A certified partner negotiates discounts for Indian clients.
