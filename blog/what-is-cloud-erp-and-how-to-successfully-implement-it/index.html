What is Cloud ERP and How to Successfully Implement It?

In 2025, businesses are racing to stay ahead, and cloud ERP is their secret weapon. But what exactly is cloud ERP, and how do you implement it without tripping over your own feet? What is Cloud ERP?

Definition and Basics

Cloud ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) is a software system hosted on the cloud—think Microsoft Azure—managing core business functions: finance, inventory, production, and more. Unlike on-site ERP, it’s accessed online, cutting hardware costs and boosting flexibility. Business Central cloud ERP, for instance, integrates all this into one scalable hub.

Why Cloud ERP Matters in 2025

Legacy ERP ties you to servers and IT crews—cloud ERP solutions free you up. A 2023 Forrester study pegged Business Central’s ROI at 172%, driven by its scalable cloud ERP model. “Cloud ERP benefits” searches reflect its rise—80% of ERP adopters now lean cloud (Gartner 2024).
