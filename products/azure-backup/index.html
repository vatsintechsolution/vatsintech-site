Azure Backup

Azure Backup offers a reliable and cost-effective solution to protect your data, whether it's in the cloud or on-premises. Simplify your backup strategy and ensure business continuity.

Request a Demo

Why Choose Azure Backup?

  • Enterprise-Grade Protection

    Azure Backup provides secure, reliable, and compliant protection for mission-critical data across cloud and on-prem environments.

  • Simple & Centralized Management

    Monitor, manage, and automate your entire backup infrastructure from a single dashboard.

  • Cost-Effective & Scalable

    Pay only for the storage you use and scale seamlessly as your backup needs grow.

Key Features of Azure Backup

  • Centralized Management

    Manage backups for all workloads from a single, unified interface.

  • Flexible Retention Policies

    Customize retention settings to meet compliance and business needs.

  • Secure Data Encryption

    Ensure protection with encryption at rest and in transit, including support for customer-managed keys.

  • Application-Consistent Backups

    Ensure transaction-consistent backups for apps like SQL Server, SharePoint, and SAP.

  • Instant File Recovery

    Quickly restore individual files or folders without requiring a full VM restore.

  • Long-Term Backup & Archiving

    Store backups for years using Azure Backup Vault and archive tiers at low cost.
